Frequently asked questions

The quick answers

Do I need an account to book?

No. Browse and book as a guest. Guest checkout asks for your name, email, and mobile number.

What does the platform fee cover?

A fixed 5 EGP platform fee is added per ticket. The cinema ticket price, fee, and total are shown before payment.

How do I use my ticket?

Open your scannable ticket from the confirmation page or email and show it at the cinema entrance.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

The relevant cinema’s policy applies and is shown before purchase. Exact cancellation, refund, and show-change workflows are agreed with each cinema. Contact support if a cinema changes or cancels your show.

What if my seats become unavailable?

Availability is checked again before your seats are reserved. If a seat is no longer available, change your quantity or arrangement to refresh matching showtimes; the platform won't silently substitute near matches.

Which tickets can be resold?

Only unused tickets originally purchased through YALLA can be listed. Sellers need an account and verified payout details; scanned or used tickets are not eligible.

How are resale prices and fees calculated?

A seller's price cannot exceed the original cinema ticket amount paid, excluding platform fees. Buyers pay the listing price plus 5 EGP per ticket. Sellers pay a 20 EGP fee only after a successful transfer; proceeds cannot be negative.

What happens if a resale doesn't sell or a transfer fails?

Unsold listings close at showtime. The original ticket stays blocked until the cinema confirms reactivation. If buyer payment succeeds but ticket transfer fails, the resale is cancelled, the buyer is refunded automatically, and the seller's original ticket remains valid.
